✦ ABOUT INTERVIA ✦

Prepare Better.
Perform With Confidence.

Intervia is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers practice interviews, understand their performance, and become more confident candidates.

Practice. Perform. Progress.

WHY INTERVIA?

Turn interview practice into real progress.

Preparing for an interview can be difficult when you don't know what questions you will face or whether your answers are good enough.

Intervia gives you a safe place to practice. Its AI interviewer creates realistic interview sessions, analyzes your performance, and gives you useful suggestions after every attempt.

Instead of simply practicing more, you can understand what to improve and why.

✦ WHAT WE FOCUS ON ✦

Built around your growth.

Intervia focuses on the parts of interview preparation that matter most.

Realistic Practice

Experience interview sessions designed to feel closer to real interviews.

Useful Feedback

Understand your strengths, weaknesses, and the areas that need attention.

Confidence Building

Practice repeatedly in a comfortable environment before facing the real interview.

Continuous Progress

Track your performance over time and see how your interview readiness improves.
