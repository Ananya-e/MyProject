# 🎤 Intervia

### Your AI-powered interview partner — available whenever you're ready. 🤖💼

**Intervia** is an AI mock interview platform designed to make interview practice feel more realistic, personalized, and less intimidating.

Upload your resume, choose your interview style, and let Intervia take it from there. 🚀

---

## ✨ What can you do with Intervia?

📄 **Resume-Based Preparation**  
Upload your resume and prepare for interviews based on your skills, experience, and background.

🎯 **Choose Your Target Role**  
Practice for popular job roles or enter your own custom role.

🧠 **AI-Powered Questions**  
Get interview questions designed around your selected role and experience level.

💬 **Realistic Mock Interviews**  
Answer questions just like you would in a real interview.

🎙️ **Voice Interview Mode**  
Want something closer to a real interview? Talk to Intervia using voice.

📊 **AI-Powered Evaluation**  
Get feedback on your answers, including:
- Technical Skills
- Communication
- Confidence
- Answer Structure

📈 **Track Your Progress**  
Keep an eye on your interview performance and see how you improve over time.

🗂️ **Interview History**  
Review your completed and cancelled interviews whenever you want.

👨‍💼 **Admin Dashboard**  
A dedicated dashboard provides an overview of users, interviews, completion rates, and performance.
